# src: the game

TypeScript, built by Vite into one file, `dist/index.html`. Rules from `CLAUDE.md`: systems change state, rendering only reads it, the UI only sends commands, modules announce events.

| Folder or file | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `core/` | `state.ts` (the saved game), `loop.ts` (fixed 1/30 s step), `events.ts` (event bus), `rng.ts` (seeded randomness), `clock.ts` (time of day, 12-minute days), `save.ts` (versioned saves with migrations) |
| `data/` | Tables only: `skills.ts` (skills, resources and their models, items, from the Wanderskill prototype; the tools each swing needs), `xp.ts` (the 1-99 curve), `animation.ts` (each skill's swing: clip, tool, timings and standing distance, from `assets/models/miner.json`), `hauling.ts` (backpack size), `changelog.ts` (what changed in each version, in players' words, for the "What's new" note), `goals.ts` (the goals that lead a new player through the camp), `pickups.ts` (things picked up by hand: what each gives, its model, whether it comes back) |
| `world/layout.ts` | The village (VILLAGE), in rings from the square: buildings (each kind has a ring in `RING`), props, gardens, rocks and trees to work (nodes), scenery trees, lanterns, river, roads and lanes, the land under fog |
| `world/camp.ts` | Where a new game starts (CAMP): a clearing below a waterfall pouring down a towering wall, its trees and rocks, the wreck, stones and branches to pick up, the spots where the tent and campfire will be built, the river and the falls |
| `world/water.ts` | The water as one shape: the river (a smooth channel with gently wavy banks, running on past the haze) and the camp's pool (an irregular basin opening into it); how far a point is from the water's edge, for drawing, walking and keeping trees off the bank |
| `world/falls.ts` | The foot of the camp's wall: where it stands, the scree slope against it, and the rocks (boulders, smaller rocks, rubble) and ferns on and below it; the bigger rocks block walking |
| `world/nav.ts` | Walking: a grid of where a person can stand (the water and the bigger rocks at the wall's foot block; things to pick up and rubble don't), A* paths and straight-line smoothing; everything that blocks goes here |
| `actors/player.ts` | Walking to a tap, to a rock or tree (stand within the tool's reach), to a building's door or the edge of unclaimed land, to stand beside something to pick up |
| `systems/` | `gathering.ts` (swings, yields into the backpack, rocks and trees running out and growing back, moving on), `backpack.ts` (the backpack and the bank), `hauling.ts` (a full backpack walked to the bank and back to work; tapping the bank), `skills.ts` (XP, level-ups), `pickups.ts` (picking things up by hand, the book, stones and branches coming back), `commands.ts` (the UI's only way in; refuses a tree or rock without its tool) |
| `sim.ts` | One simulation step; headless, used by the game and the tests |
| `render/` | three.js: `view.ts` (the character and his tools, the click cross), `arms.ts` (whether the tool is in his hands or on his back, and keeps his hands on the handle when clips crossfade), `nodes.ts` (rocks and trees: running out, falling, stumps, growing back, chips and leaves), `waterfall.ts` (the camp's towering wall lost in mist and cloud, falling water, foam and spray, and the foot of the mountain: scree slope, rocks, moss and ferns; how far a point is in front of the wall, for the camera), `pickups.ts` (things to pick up: hidden once taken, a glint to spot them), `world.ts` (ground, paths, the sandy bank and stony ground at the wall's foot, the river and pool as one sheet of water, running on out of sight with its own sand bank past the detailed ground), `village.ts` (buildings, trees, lanterns, hearth, night glow, merged per material), `fog.ts` (mist over unclaimed land), `lighting.ts` (day and night), `camera.ts` (third-person orbit; never goes behind the camp's wall) |
| `ui/` | `input.ts` (tap, drag, pinch, keys), `hud.ts` (panels, floating numbers), `news.ts` ("What's new" after an update; the version button), `intro.ts` (the welcome for a new player), `backpack.ts` (what's in the backpack, opened from the panel, with buttons to drop things) |
| `main.ts` | Wires it all together; `window.hearthfolk` exposes state and view for checking in the browser console |

Tests are in `tests/`; the long headless run in `tests/play.test.ts` is the start of the plan's multi-day simulation check. `tests/architecture.test.ts` enforces the module boundaries: each folder may only import from the layers listed there, the simulation layers may not touch three.js or the page, and `render/` and `ui/` may not assign to game state. If it fails, move the code to the right layer rather than loosening the rule.
