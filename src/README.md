# src: the game

TypeScript, built by Vite into one file, `dist/index.html`. Rules from `CLAUDE.md`: systems change state, rendering only reads it, the UI only sends commands, modules announce events.

| Folder or file | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `core/` | `state.ts` (the saved game), `loop.ts` (fixed 1/30 s step), `events.ts` (event bus), `rng.ts` (seeded randomness), `clock.ts` (time of day, 12-minute days; which night it is and whether it is night, for stoking the fire), `save.ts` (versioned saves with migrations; each save records the real time the game had been played up to, for away time), `book.ts` (his book's pages: one per goal up to the current one, done or current, the tick and count per line, counted as the goal card counts; with every goal done the last page says the camp is set up) |
| `data/` | Tables only: `skills.ts` (skills, resources and their models, items, from the Wanderskill prototype; the tools each swing needs), `xp.ts` (the 1-99 curve), `animation.ts` (each skill's swing: clip, tool, timings and standing distance, from `assets/models/miner.json`), `hauling.ts` (backpack size), `away.ts` (away time: a fifth of his speed while the game is closed, for at most 2 hours; a background tab at full speed; when the note shows), `fire.ts` (stoking the fire at night: nightfall, how many logs, how long; how the fire flares and burns low), `changelog.ts` (what changed in each version, in players' words, for the "What's new" note), `goals.ts` (the goals that lead a new player through the camp; gathering goals and making goals; each goal's page text for his book; what the card says once the camp is set up), `making.ts` (things he makes: what each needs, where it is made, how long it takes, what it gives: a structure such as the campfire, the shelter or the storage chest, or a tool such as the stone axe or the stone pickaxe), `pickups.ts` (things picked up by hand: what each gives, its model, whether it comes back, and which group tapping it gathers) |
| `world/layout.ts` | The village (VILLAGE), in rings from the square: buildings (each kind has a ring in `RING`), props, gardens, rocks and trees to work (nodes), scenery trees, lanterns, river, roads and lanes, the land under fog; where things are built (`siteOf`: the camp's fire spot, tent spot and chest spot) |
| `world/camp.ts` | Where a new game starts (CAMP): a clearing below a waterfall pouring down a towering wall, its trees and rocks, the wreck, stones and branches to pick up, the fire spot where the campfire is built, the tent spot where the shelter is and the chest spot beside it, the river and the falls |
| `world/water.ts` | The water as one shape: the river (a smooth channel with gently wavy banks, running on past the haze) and the camp's pool (an irregular basin opening into it); how far a point is from the water's edge, for drawing, walking and keeping trees off the bank |
| `world/falls.ts` | The foot of the camp's wall: where it stands, the scree slope against it, and the rocks (boulders, smaller rocks, rubble) and ferns on and below it; the bigger rocks block walking |
| `world/nav.ts` | Walking: a grid of where a person can stand (the water and the bigger rocks at the wall's foot block; things to pick up and rubble don't), A* paths and straight-line smoothing; everything that blocks goes here, what he has built too (one grid per set of built things; the campfire as a circle, the tent and the chest by their own width and depth, turned like a building) |
| `actors/player.ts` | Walking to a tap, to a rock or tree (stand within the tool's reach), to a building's door or the edge of unclaimed land, to stand beside something to pick up, beside where he builds, or beside something he built (the chest, the campfire) |
| `systems/` | `gathering.ts` (swings, yields into the backpack, rocks and trees running out and growing back, moving on), `backpack.ts` (the backpack and what is stored: the bank, or the camp's chest; where the backpack can be emptied), `hauling.ts` (a full backpack walked to the bank or the camp's chest and back to work or gathering; tapping the bank or the chest; going back to what he was doing), `fire.ts` (stoking the fire at nightfall with logs from the chest or the backpack, once a night; the fire burning low with no logs), `skills.ts` (XP, level-ups), `away.ts` (how much play time away is worth, the game closed or left open (a background tab, the screen off); the real time between frames, by the wall clock after the page was hidden, for a phone asleep; what he did meanwhile, counted from the events, and what the note tells), `pickups.ts` (picking things up by hand, the book, stones and branches coming back; gathering: a tapped plank, stone or branch leads on to the nearest of its group that fits in the backpack, waiting for stones and branches to come back), `making.ts` (making things: he works beside the site or where he stands, then the needs are taken from the backpack, then the bank, and a structure stands or he owns a tool), `goals.ts` (writes finished goals down, so they stay finished), `commands.ts` (the UI's only way in; refuses a tree or rock without its tool, and making something at the camp before the goals before it are done, or without everything it needs, saying what comes first or what's missing; a stone or branch tapped with a full backpack goes to the bank or the chest first where there is one; a command stops a trip or stoking the fire, except a tap on the chest that sends him where he was going) |
| `sim.ts` | One simulation step; headless, used by the game and the tests; catching up many steps in a row (a background tab, or away time when a save is opened, never on the artifact viewer's hot swap) and counting what he did |
| `render/` | three.js: `view.ts` (the character and his tools, at the camp the axe and pick drawn as the stone axe and stone pickaxe, a placeholder bow while he builds or makes something or stokes the fire, the click cross), `arms.ts` (whether the tool is in his hands or on his back, and keeps his hands on the handle when clips crossfade), `nodes.ts` (rocks and trees: running out, falling, stumps, growing back, chips and leaves), `waterfall.ts` (the camp's towering wall lost in mist and cloud, falling water, foam and spray, and the foot of the mountain: scree slope, rocks, moss and ferns; how far a point is in front of the wall, for the camera), `structures.ts` (what he builds: the campfire with flickering flames, smoke and a warm light, flaring up when stoked and burning low with no logs, the shelter, a tent facing the fire, and the storage chest, built in code, which a tap stores things in; until then each spot's faint ring with a faint outline of the thing inside, the ring filling up while he builds), `pickups.ts` (things to pick up: hidden once taken, a glint to spot them, a faint one inside a ring that fills up where he waits for a stone or branch to come back), `world.ts` (ground, paths, the sandy bank and stony ground at the wall's foot, the river and pool as one sheet of water, running on out of sight with its own sand bank past the detailed ground), `village.ts` (buildings, trees, lanterns, hearth, night glow, merged per material), `fog.ts` (mist over unclaimed land), `lighting.ts` (day and night), `camera.ts` (third-person orbit; never goes behind the camp's wall); placeholder models for chapter 2's later chunks, not in play yet: `creatures.ts` (the rat, built in code, and the goblin, a smaller green copy of the character with a club; each posed every frame: idle, moving, attacking, knocked over; a bite or swing can keep to the caller's attack clock), `gear.ts` (hide armour on the character's bones: cap, vest, shield, shown or hidden), `props.ts` (the rats' nest; the goblin camp's palisade, hide tent and totem), `parts.ts` (for models built in code from many parts: one shared material per colour, and merging a model's parts into one mesh per material), `showcase.ts` (a dev-only look at them all near the start, with `?models` in the page's address) |
| `ui/` | `input.ts` (tap, drag, pinch, keys), `hud.ts` (panels, the storage line (Bank, or Chest at the camp), floating numbers, the goal card and its Build button, and what it says once the camp is set up), `book.ts` (his book: a page per goal over the game, opened when he picks it up or by tapping the goal card), `news.ts` ("What's new" after an update; the version button), `away.ts` ("While you were away": how long, how much of it he worked, what he gathered, the XP and levels, the fire; it waits while "What's new", the welcome or his book is open), `intro.ts` (the welcome for a new player), `backpack.ts` (what's in the backpack, opened from the panel, with buttons to drop things) |
| `main.ts` | Wires it all together; catches up away time when a save is opened, and saves with the time it was played up to; `window.hearthfolk` exposes state, view, the frame clock and the away time caught up, for checking in the browser console; `?models` in the address adds the dev-only look at chapter 2's models (`render/showcase.ts`) |

Tests are in `tests/`; the long headless run in `tests/play.test.ts` is the start of the plan's multi-day simulation check. `tests/architecture.test.ts` enforces the module boundaries: each folder may only import from the layers listed there, the simulation layers may not touch three.js or the page, and `render/` and `ui/` may not assign to game state. If it fails, move the code to the right layer rather than loosening the rule.
