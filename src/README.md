# src: the game

TypeScript, built by Vite into one file, `dist/index.html`. Rules from `CLAUDE.md`: systems change state, rendering only reads it, the UI only sends commands, modules announce events.

| Folder or file | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `core/` | `state.ts` (the saved game), `loop.ts` (fixed 1/30 s step), `events.ts` (event bus), `rng.ts` (seeded randomness), `clock.ts` (time of day, 12-minute days; which night it is and whether it is night, for stoking the fire), `save.ts` (versioned saves with migrations; each save records the real time the game had been played up to, for away time), `book.ts` (his book's pages: one per goal up to the current one, done or current, the tick and count per line, counted as the goal card counts, a skill level counted as "Attack level 2 / 3"; with every goal done the last page says the camp is set up), `targets.ts` (what he can fight: the training dummy once built, and the rats, each with its health and whether it is down; where it is, how big, how well it defends, what it teaches and up to which level; the hover's words for it), `rats.ts` (the rats round the layout's nests: their ids, the nest each lives round and its home spot by the hole, a rat as it starts and comes back, a saved rat read back) |
| `data/` | Tables only: `skills.ts` (skills, resources and their models, items, from the Wanderskill prototype; the tools each swing needs), `xp.ts` (the 1-99 curve), `animation.ts` (each skill's swing: clip, tool, timings and standing distance, from `assets/models/miner.json`), `hauling.ts` (backpack size), `away.ts` (away time: a fifth of his speed while the game is closed, for at most 2 hours; a background tab at full speed; when the note shows), `fire.ts` (stoking the fire at night: nightfall, how many logs, how long; how the fire flares and burns low), `changelog.ts` (what changed in each version, in players' words, for the "What's new" note), `goals.ts` (the goals that lead a new player through the camp; gathering goals and making goals; goals that ask for skill levels (the practice goal); each goal's page text for his book; what the card says once the camp is set up), `making.ts` (things he makes: what each needs, where it is made, how long it takes, what it gives: a structure such as the campfire, the shelter or the storage chest, a tool such as the stone axe, the stone pickaxe or the fishing rod, or gear he wears such as the stone club or the hide armour, stitched from rat hides), `combat.ts` (his health by Vitality level and how fast it comes back; the training styles; gear and its slots, bonuses and weapon speed, the stone club and the hide armour (a cap, a vest and a shield: defence only); the strike (the axe's Chop swing) and its timer; the training dummy's numbers), `food.ts` (what heals and by how much), `cooking.ts` (cooking fish at the campfire: how long each takes, its XP, and the chance one burns, less as his Cooking level rises), `pickups.ts` (things picked up by hand: what each gives, its model, whether it comes back, and which group tapping it gathers; the berry bushes, which stay standing when picked), `rats.ts` (a rat's numbers: health, its bite and how often, what it leaves, when it comes back; how it potters about its nest; its bite's timing, which the rat model is drawn by; eating in a fight, the food taken from the chest, and being knocked out) |
| `world/layout.ts` | The village (VILLAGE), in rings from the square: buildings (each kind has a ring in `RING`), props, gardens, rocks and trees to work (nodes), scenery trees, lanterns, river, roads and lanes, the land under fog; where things are built (`siteOf`: the camp's fire spot, tent spot, chest spot, firewood pile spot and training dummy spot); rats' nests (`nests`) |
| `world/camp.ts` | Where a new game starts (CAMP): a clearing below a waterfall pouring down a towering wall, its trees and rocks, the wreck, stones and branches to pick up, the berry bushes east of the fire, the fishing spots in the pool and along the river, the fire spot where the campfire is built, the tent spot where the shelter is and the chest spot beside it, the training dummy's spot west of the fire, the rats' nest in the edge of the woods downriver, the river and the falls |
| `world/water.ts` | The water as one shape: the river (a smooth channel with gently wavy banks, running on past the haze) and the camp's pool (an irregular basin opening into it); how far a point is from the water's edge, for drawing, walking and keeping trees off the bank |
| `world/falls.ts` | The foot of the camp's wall: where it stands, the scree slope against it, and the rocks (boulders, smaller rocks, rubble) and ferns on and below it; the bigger rocks block walking |
| `world/nav.ts` | Walking: a grid of where a person can stand (the water, the bigger rocks at the wall's foot, the camp's berry bushes and the rats' nest block; things lying on the ground and rubble don't), A* paths and straight-line smoothing; everything that blocks goes here, what he has built too (one grid per set of built things; the campfire and the training dummy as circles, the tent and the chest by their own width and depth, turned like a building) |
| `actors/player.ts` | Walking to a tap, to a rock, tree or fishing spot (stand within the tool's reach), to a building's door or the edge of unclaimed land, to stand beside something to pick up, beside where he builds, beside something he built (the chest, the campfire), or within striking reach of something to fight (the training dummy) |
| `systems/` | `gathering.ts` (swings, yields into the backpack, rocks and trees running out and growing back, fishing spots going quiet for a while, moving on), `backpack.ts` (the backpack and what is stored: the bank, or the camp's chest; where the backpack can be emptied), `hauling.ts` (a full backpack walked to the bank or the camp's chest and back to work or gathering; tapping the bank or the chest; going back to what he was doing), `fire.ts` (stoking the fire at nightfall with logs from the chest or the backpack, once a night; the fire burning low with no logs), `skills.ts` (XP, level-ups), `away.ts` (how much play time away is worth, the game closed or left open (a background tab, the screen off); the real time between frames, by the wall clock after the page was hidden, for a phone asleep; what he did meanwhile, counted from the events, and what the note tells), `pickups.ts` (picking things up by hand, the book, stones and branches coming back; gathering: a tapped plank, stone or branch leads on to the nearest of its group that fits in the backpack, waiting for stones and branches to come back; the berry bushes are picked the same way and stay standing, bare until their berries grow back, and a tap on a bare one sends him to wait beside it), `making.ts` (making things: he works beside the site or where he stands, then the needs are taken from the backpack, then the bank, and a structure stands, he owns a tool or he wears gear (the stone club)), `goals.ts` (writes finished goals down, so they stay finished), `food.ts` (eating food from the backpack heals him, never past his most), `cooking.ts` (cooking the raw fish he has at the campfire one after another, fetching them from the chest when he carries none; stoking the fire at night breaks off and comes back to it), `combat.ts` (fighting as in OSRS: the accuracy roll, max hit and XP as small formulas; practising on the training dummy, a strike each time his weapon comes round until he is sent elsewhere; the Train choice; the dummy's level limit; his health coming back; a strike takes a rat's health; his defence roll (his Defence level, the defensive style and the armour he wears), which anything that bites or hits him rolls against), `rats.ts` (the rats round their nest: pottering about, never going for him; a rat he fights stays put and bites back on its own timer; beaten, it topples, maybe leaving a hide, and comes back a minute later; he goes on to the next rat or waits by the nest; eating when hurt; setting off by the chest for food; knocked out, he wakes at the camp by the shelter), `commands.ts` (the UI's only way in; refuses a tree, rock or fishing spot without its tool (axe, pickaxe, rod) before he walks, and making something at the camp before the goals before it are done, or without everything it needs, saying what comes first or what's missing; a stone, branch or berry bush tapped with a full backpack goes to the bank or the chest first where there is one; a tap on the campfire cooks, or with no raw fish says so (before he owns a rod it only walks him beside the fire); a tap on the training dummy practises on it, or says he needs a weapon; a command stops a trip or stoking the fire, except a tap on the chest that sends him where he was going) |
| `sim.ts` | One simulation step; headless, used by the game and the tests; catching up many steps in a row (a background tab, or away time when a save is opened, never on the artifact viewer's hot swap) and counting what he did |
| `render/` | three.js: `view.ts` (the character and his tools, at the camp the axe and pick drawn as the stone axe and stone pickaxe, the rod only while he fishes, his stone club swung with the Chop clip while he practises, the hide armour he wears (`wearGear`), a placeholder bow while he builds or makes something or stokes the fire, the click cross), `gear.ts` (what he wears, drawn in code: a model for each weapon, the stone club; and the hide armour, a cap, a vest and a shield put on his bones by `wearGear`; `armourWorn` picks them from what he wears), `creatures.ts` (the rat, built in code, and the goblin, a smaller green copy of the character with a club; each posed every frame: idle, moving, attacking, knocked over; a bite or swing can keep to the caller's attack clock), `rats.ts` (the rats' nest and the rats in play, posed from what the simulation says each is doing, gone a moment after they are beaten until they come back; a tap area on each), `props.ts` (the rats' nest; the goblin camp's palisade, hide tent and totem), `parts.ts` (for models built in code from many parts: one shared material per colour, and merging a model's parts into one mesh per material), `showcase.ts` (a dev-only look at them all near the start, with `?models` in the page's address), `arms.ts` (whether the tool is in his hands or on his back, and keeps his hands on the handle when clips crossfade), `nodes.ts` (rocks and trees: running out, falling, stumps, growing back, chips and leaves), `waterfall.ts` (the camp's towering wall lost in mist and cloud, falling water, foam and spray, and the foot of the mountain: scree slope, rocks, moss and ferns; how far a point is in front of the wall, for the camera), `structures.ts` (what he builds: the campfire with flickering flames, smoke and a warm light, flaring up when stoked and burning low with no logs, which a tap cooks at once he owns a rod or has raw fish, the shelter, a tent facing the fire, the storage chest, built in code, which a tap stores things in, and the training dummy, built in code, which a tap sends him to practise on and which wobbles when struck; until then each spot's faint ring with a faint outline of the thing inside, the ring filling up while he builds), `pickups.ts` (things to pick up: hidden once taken, a glint to spot them, a faint one inside a ring that fills up where he waits for a stone or branch to come back), `bushes.ts` (the berry bushes, built in code, their berries showing while ripe), `fishing.ts` (the fishing spots' ripples, the rod he fishes with, its line and bobbing float), `cooking.ts` (a fish on a stick over the campfire while he cooks), `world.ts` (ground, paths, the sandy bank and stony ground at the wall's foot, the river and pool as one sheet of water, running on out of sight with its own sand bank past the detailed ground), `village.ts` (buildings, trees, lanterns, hearth, night glow, merged per material), `fog.ts` (mist over unclaimed land), `lighting.ts` (day and night), `camera.ts` (third-person orbit; never goes behind the camp's wall) |
| `ui/` | `input.ts` (tap, drag, pinch, keys), `windows.ts` (the windows the row of buttons opens, along the bottom on a phone held upright and down the right side otherwise: each closes with its x, its button or Esc; one at a time on a phone, two side by side on a wide screen while they fit clear of the goal card; where one would cover the goal card (a tablet held upright, a phone held sideways) it starts under the card, or on a short screen stands between the card and the buttons, narrower (`windowSpot`), so the Build button stays in sight; a window is a `section.win[data-win]` in `index.html` with a `[data-opens]` button, so a new one is one more of each; the notes over the game share the look; it keeps the stage's `--below-card`, `--above-bottom`, `--free-width`, `--wins-top` and `--wins-left` up to date for notices and windows), `hud.ts` (the always-on screen: the goal card in one line or two with its Build or Make button while he can make the thing (a tap opens his book, or before he has it says the hint), what it says once the camp is set up, the strip's skill gaining XP with its level and a thin bar, floating numbers, notices (eating and cooking among them)), `backpack.ts` (the Backpack window: his 28 slots as a grid, 7 across and 4 down, one thing in each, each kind together; a tapped thing lights up its kind and the bar under the grid says what it is and how many, with Eat for food, Drop one and Drop all, or why he keeps it (the wreck's things); what he wears; what is stored (Bank, or Chest at the camp) and the firewood pile, each thing with its picture and how many; the Backpack button's count), `pictures.ts` (pictures drawn in code: one for every item, every piece of gear and, faintly, each empty place gear goes, and an icon for every skill; a plain bundle for anything without one yet), `skills.ts` (the Skills window: every skill he has started with its icon, its level, a bar and the XP to the next level, his total level at the foot; above them the Train choice with a line on what the style picked does), `book.ts` (his book in a window: a page per goal, opened when he picks it up, from its button once he has it, or by tapping the goal card), `settings.ts` (the Settings window: text size, remembered in this browser; the version and What's new; Start over, two taps), `news.ts` ("What's new" after an update; the version button), `away.ts` ("While you were away": how long, how much of it he worked, what he gathered, the fish he cooked, the XP and levels, the fire, practice stopped at the training dummy's level limit; it waits while "What's new", the welcome or his book is open), `intro.ts` (the welcome for a new player), `armour.ts` (what he wears, in the Backpack window once he wears anything: a tile for each place gear goes, weapon, head, body and shield, with its picture and name, and his attack, strength and defence bonuses from all of it), `combat.ts` (his health in the strip and a small bar over his head while hurt; the Train choice in the Skills window; the numbers over what he strikes; the notices that go with fighting), `rats.ts` (a bar over a hurt rat, the numbers over a rat he strikes and over him when bitten, and the notices of fighting rats: food from the chest, waking at camp) |
| `main.ts` | Wires it all together; catches up away time when a save is opened, and saves with the time it was played up to; `window.hearthfolk` exposes state, view, the frame clock and the away time caught up, for checking in the browser console; `?models` in the address adds the dev-only look at chapter 2's models (`render/showcase.ts`) |

Tests are in `tests/`; the long headless run in `tests/play.test.ts` is the start of the plan's multi-day simulation check. `tests/architecture.test.ts` enforces the module boundaries: each folder may only import from the layers listed there, the simulation layers may not touch three.js or the page, and `render/` and `ui/` may not assign to game state. If it fails, move the code to the right layer rather than loosening the rule.
