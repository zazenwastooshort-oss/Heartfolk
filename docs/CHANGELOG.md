A sweep of 22,680 camera positions along the north edge stays in front, never nearer him than 2 m. (2) Tapping across the river walks him to the near bank opposite, not 17 m down it: `NavGrid.nearestWalkable` keeps searching rings until no closer cell can be left, and also works for points far off the grid; a cell by the river is open only if all of it is dry land. (3, 5, 6) A full backpack can always be made room in at the camp: tap the backpack in the panel to see what's in it and drop one or all of something (`ui/backpack.ts`, the `drop` command, `systems/backpack.ts`, events `dropped` and `cantDrop`); the wreck's planks and tarp can't be dropped, since they never come back (`canDrop` in `core/state.ts`), and they can never fill the backpack themselves. The "backpack full" notices say to open it. A 0.0.11 camp save left chopping until full keeps its logs and can drop them; the migration is unchanged. (4) The mist, the cloud and the water coming out of the mist moved down to where a low, zoomed-out camera sees them (mist from 5 m, half by 11 m, 0.8 by 18 m; cloud 11-30 m; the water fades in from 18 to 34 m); the mist thins out near the camera so a high camera by the wall doesn't look down through it. (7) The goal card's choice is `currentGoal` in `core/state.ts`, used by the HUD and the tests. (8) The river test now checks the whole bank: the last walkable step is on dry land within 0.8 m of the water, and points just inside the water are not walkable. 143 tests pass. |
