| 11 Oct 2026 | Game 0.0.8 | Mining moves on like woodcutting (the owner asked why he stood at an empty rock): when a rock runs out he pauses half a second, then walks to the nearest rock with ore if that beats waiting, so he hops between the copper rocks. Each resource now says how long the pause is (`moveOnAfter` in `data/skills.ts`: rocks 0.5 s, trees 1 s so the fall is seen), and the margin a walk must win by dropped from 1 s to 0.3 s. 78 tests pass. |
| 11 Oct 2026 | Game 0.0.7 | The owner's feedback on 0.0.6. **Less downtime:** when a tree falls (or a rock runs out) the character waits a second to see it land, then walks to the nearest free one of the same kind, but only when that beats waiting for it to grow back, so he stays at the copper rocks, which regrow in 2.6 s. He never walks into land not yet claimed, and a stump you tap yourself is one he waits at. **Arms that bend:** each arm now has an upper arm and a forearm; the hands hold the handle and the elbows bend on the wind-up and straighten into the strike (a two-bone IK baked per frame in `export_miner_glb.py`). The tool lands exactly where it did, so timings and standing distances are unchanged. The mining wind-up now goes up over the right shoulder like the chop (raised straight up, the hands hid the face). A new `src/render/arms.ts` re-solves the arms after every animation blend, which would otherwise take the hands off the handle (up to 16 cm when arriving at a tree). **Twice as fast:** the character jogs at 2.9 m/s with a new Run animation (longer stride, leaning in), played at the rate that keeps the feet from sliding. **Camera:** dragging sideways now turns the scene the way the finger moves, as in most games. Plan: decisions on gathering downtime, the backpack (empties itself at the bank), progress rates (1x while the game is open, even in the background; 0.2x while closed, for at most 2 hours), and land (how it's obtained is open again); GitHub recorded as decided. `cinematic.py` keeps the hands on the handle through its blends too (the video itself isn't re-rendered). 78 tests pass. |
| 11 Oct 2026 | Game 0.0.6 | Step 2, woodcutting. Six birch trees in the woods south of the square (Woodcutting level 1) and three oaks by the river (level 10), with the prototype's values (birch 2.2 s, 10 xp; oak 2.8 s, 22 xp). Tap a tree and the character takes out an axe and chops: a diagonal swing over the right shoulder that bites the trunk at waist height. `export_miner_glb.py` adds the axe and the Chop animation and works out where to stand so the blade meets the trunk (further back from thicker trunks); `-- check` renders the chop against a trunk. A felled tree topples away from the woodcutter and leaves a stump (new StumpBirch and StumpOak models), then grows back; each strike throws wood chips, knocks leaves loose and shakes the tree. The skills panel shows both skills, built from `data/skills.ts`. Hover text says Chop or Mine; trying an oak too early says which level it needs. Rocks and trees are drawn by the new `render/nodes.ts`. Save format 3 (the mining task became a general gathering task; older saves carry on). Fixed: after the game was in the background, every strike made while catching up threw its own burst of chips at once; now one burst is shown. Decorative trees inside the village are now pines, so no birch or oak there looks choppable when it isn't. 64 tests pass. |
