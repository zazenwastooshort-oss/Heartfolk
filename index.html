      Welcome to Hearthfolk

      The river threw you over the falls, a very long way down. You washed up on this bank with nothing, not even your tools.

      Something wrecked came down with you. Its pieces are strewn along the bank.

      • Tap a plank and you pick up every plank, the two in the smashed crate too. Tap the torn tarp and your book to pick them up.
      • Tap a stone or a branch and you keep gathering them until your backpack is full.
      • The goal at the top shows what's next. Tap it and he sets off to do it. The buttons at the edge open your backpack, your skills and the settings.
